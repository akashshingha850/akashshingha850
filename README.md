### Akash Shingha Bappy

**Robotics · Computer Vision · Edge AI** — Project Researcher, [Biomimetics and Intelligent Systems Group](https://www.oulu.fi/en/research-groups/biomimetics-and-intelligent-systems-group), University of Oulu, Finland

I build robots that see and fly on their own, and take perception models from raw data to field deployment on embedded hardware. Previously a research trainee at VTT Technical Research Centre of Finland.

[Portfolio](https://akashshingha850.github.io) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/akashshingha) · [Publications](https://akashshingha850.github.io/#publications)

#### What I work on

- **Autonomous UAVs:** GPS-denied flight with PX4, visual-inertial odometry and EKF2 fusion, onboard mission logic
- **Digital twins:** Isaac Sim + Pegasus twins where simulated and real drones run the same ROS 2 containers
- **Perception & datasets:** detection and segmentation on RGB and LiDAR, dataset design and benchmarks
- **Edge deployment:** ONNX / TensorRT / DeepStream on NVIDIA Jetson for real-time, on-device inference
